Privacy Policy for AI Glance

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

1. Information We Collect

AI Glance captures user-initiated screen regions and images solely to perform structured data extraction and optical character recognition (OCR) requested by the user.

2. How Data is Processed

Captured screen captures are securely transmitted to our backend API to perform AI-driven extraction. We do not store, log, or sell your image captures or extracted data. Once processing is complete, the response is delivered back to your device and discarded from transient server memory.

3. Third-Party Services

AI Glance utilizes secure cloud AI inference providers to extract text and tabular structures from images. These third parties process data under strict data privacy and retention agreements.

4. Data Retention and Security

We do not retain permanent records of captured screen content or personal user data. All data transmissions between the application and our servers are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

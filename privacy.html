Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 12, 2026

In short: AI Glance only looks at your screen when you press the shortcut, sends that capture straight to Google’s Gemini API using your own API key, and keeps your settings and history on your own computer.

1. Information we collect

AI Glance captures a screen image only when you trigger it (Ctrl + Shift + Space), solely to extract structured data (optical character recognition and table extraction) that you requested. We do not run background screen monitoring.

2. How your data is processed

  • Your capture, and any instruction you type into the AI command bar, is sent directly from your device to Google’s Gemini API using the Google AI Studio API key that you provide.
  • We do not operate servers that receive, store, log or sell your screen captures, extracted data or API key.
  • The results are shown in the app as a Table, CSV and JSON on your device.

3. Data stored on your device

Your API key, theme preference and recent extraction history are stored locally on your computer. You can clear your key in the app’s settings, or remove all local data by uninstalling the app.

4. Third-party services

  • Google (Gemini API): processes the images and text you send under Google’s own terms and privacy policy, which may differ for free and paid API usage. Please review them before capturing sensitive information.
  • Microsoft Store: downloads and installs are handled by Microsoft under Microsoft’s privacy statement.

5. Be mindful of what is on your screen

A capture includes everything visible in the captured area, which may contain personal, financial or confidential details. Avoid capturing content you are not comfortable sending to Google.

6. Security and retention

We do not retain captured content or personal data. Data sent to Google travels over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Your API key stays on your device, so please keep it private.

7. No sale of data, no advertising

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

8. Future changes

If we add an optional paid plan for unlimited usage, we will update this policy before it launches to describe any new data involved, such as payment details handled by a payment provider.

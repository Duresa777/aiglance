Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: AI Glance only looks at your screen when you press the shortcut. The area you select is sent through our server to Google’s Gemini AI to be read, and it is not stored. You don’t need an account, and we never see your payment details.

1. What AI Glance captures

AI Glance captures a screen image only when you trigger it (with the shortcut or the capture button), solely to extract the structured data you asked for. If you select an area, only that area is sent; the rest of your screen stays on your computer. We do not run background screen monitoring.

2. How your captures are processed

  • Your capture, and any instruction you type into the AI command bar, is sent over an encrypted connection to the AI Glance server (hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards it to Google’s Gemini API and returns the result to your device.
  • The server passes captures through and does not store the images, the instructions or the extracted data.
  • If you add your own Google AI key (Settings → Advanced), your captures and instructions go directly from your device to Google’s Gemini API under your own Google account and Google’s terms. They do not pass through our server and are not counted by it.
  • The results are shown in the app, where you can copy them or save them as a file on your own computer.

3. What our server stores

To provide the free monthly allowance and Glance Pro, the server keeps a small amount of information that does not identify you by name:

  • An anonymous installation ID, a random code created by the app on your computer.
  • How many captures that installation has used in the current month and day.
  • A capture count linked to a one-way scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, used only to prevent abuse of the free allowance.
  • Whether a Glance Pro subscription is active for that installation, and when that status expires.

We do not ask for your name or email address, and the app has no account or sign-in. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as request times and error messages) to keep the service running; these do not contain your captures.

4. Data stored on your device

Your settings (theme, shortcut, capture mode), the anonymous installation ID, your recent extraction history and, if you add one, your own Google AI key (encrypted with Windows’ built-in protection) are stored locally on your computer. You can clear the history in the app, and uninstalling the app removes all local data.

5. Subscriptions and payments

Glance Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles the purchase, billing and refunds under Microsoft’s own terms and privacy statement. We do not receive your card details or your Microsoft account password. The app only learns from Windows whether a Glance Pro subscription is active.

6. Third-party services

  • Google (Gemini API): reads the images and text sent for extraction, under Google’s API terms and privacy policy. If you use your own key, Google’s terms for your account apply, which may differ for free and paid Google keys.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the AI Glance server and its usage database.
  • Microsoft Store: handles downloads, installs and subscriptions.

7. Be mindful of what is on your screen

A capture includes everything visible in the area you select, which may contain personal, financial or confidential details. Select only what you need, and avoid capturing content you are not comfortable sending to an online AI service.

8. Security and retention

Data travels over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. Captures and results are not retained on our server. Usage counts and subscription status are kept only as long as needed to run the service and prevent abuse.

9. No sale of data, no advertising

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

10. Your choices

You can stop all processing at any time by not using the capture shortcut or by uninstalling the app. To ask about, or request deletion of, the usage record linked to your installation, email us at the address below.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how AI Glance handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect.
